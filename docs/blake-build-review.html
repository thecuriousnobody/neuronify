How to test the whole thing

Go to neuronify.ai/desk and sign in with public_works-2026 (it owns open cases). Then:

  1. All cases tab — try the filters, Export CSV, and click a row to open the right-side detail.
  2. Open a case → Form tab — Approve with a "what work was completed?" note; it stays on the case and the step turns green.
  3. Workflow tab — reassign to another department with a reason; it leaves your queue and lands in theirs.
  4. Chat-log tab — file a fresh report at /report, confirm it at /desk/intake, then the resident's original words appear here.

⚠ Live shares one database with local — everything you do is real data, so use throwaway cases for anything that writes.
