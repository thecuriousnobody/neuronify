Reports now file themselves — straight to the owning department.

Everything below is live on neuronify.ai and was verified end-to-end today, twice: on a preview deployment before merge, then the full suite on production. This page is your second-hand testing guide — what shipped, what we verified, what to try yourself, and what's still open on your side.

What shipped today
  • Route-direct filing actually works, photo included. A validation bug meant every photo-carrying report was rejected at submit (the photo uploaded fine; the server dropped its URL). Found by the browser suite this morning, fixed, and verified through the Public Works queue the same day.
  • Photo policy — your item B — went conservative. The 8 photo-critical categories still require a photo, but a resident who can't provide one records a reason instead and the report files; the crew sees “No photo — resident said: …” on the record. Nobody is ever turned away. The hard wall is a one-line revert if you want it back — this needs your ratification.
  • The agent can no longer claim it filed a report. Caught live: mid-conversation it once said “I'm sending this to our street repair team right now” with fields still missing. Now every unfinished turn must end with a question — enforced in code, not just prompt.
  • Location quality. Descriptor phrases (“north side median”) no longer derail the geocoder; a match outside Peoria is discarded rather than pinned (one test pinned Rome, IL — 15 miles out); when the geocoder has alternates, the resident gets tap-able “Not this spot?” addresses.
  • Quick-reply chips. The agent offers likely answers as one-tap chips on most turns — its own predictions, not just listed choices. Typing always still works.
  • Polish. The chat wears /report's face (brand mark + “What needs fixing?” hero); the view follows the conversation down to the composer; the desk passcode field has a show/hide eye; duplicate “Where is it?” labels are gone.
  • The old doors are unlinked. Per your retirement decision: every resident-facing link now points at /report/chat, and the desk no longer advertises the Front-desk console. Both old routes still answer at their URLs until the bridge formally closes.
